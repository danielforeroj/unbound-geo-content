# Example carousel (9 slides)

Built from `sample-report.md` (fictional). Same idea as the video, in reference form.

**Style.** Dark background, one accent color, framework name "The 4 Line Page". Little text. Every content slide has a diagram.
**CTA word.** SATURDAY.

---

## Slide 1 | Cover

**Headline.** 0 of 10. A 4.9 star clinic ChatGPT never named.
**Under it.** Series tag "Get named" and "Swipe".
**Diagram.** Ten empty dots in a row, outlined, none filled. "0 of 10" large in accent.
**Design note.** Number in accent, headline off white, left aligned.

## Slide 2 | The gap

**Headline.** Same city. Two very different counts.
**Diagram.** Two horizontal bars to scale. The short bar is labeled "This clinic, 6 of 50 (12%)". The long bar is labeled "Another clinic, 31 of 50 (62%)". Accent on the long bar.
**Text.** "ChatGPT, 5 questions, 10 runs each."
**Design note.** Bars take two thirds of the slide.

## Slide 3 | The clue

**Headline.** One page did the work.
**Diagram.** Flow, left to right. Box "Saturday emergency dentist" to box "10 answers" to box "7 cite one page". The last box carries a page icon labeled "Emergency dentist".
**Text.** "Cited in 7 of 10 answers."
**Design note.** Arrows in accent. Boxes in a lighter dark.

## Slide 4 | The framework

**Headline.** The 4 Line Page.
**Diagram.** A vertical stack of four lines, all four in full, like a page outline.
1. Saturday hours, as plain text
2. What counts as an emergency
3. A price range
4. A phone number answered on Saturday
**Design note.** Framework name in accent. The stack is the slide people screenshot, so give it room.

## Slide 5 | The part people get wrong

**Headline.** Hours in a picture are invisible.
**Diagram.** Before and after. Left, a footer with the hours as an image, crossed out and labeled "Image". Right, the same hours as a text line, labeled "Plain text".
**Design note.** Left muted, right in accent.

## Slide 6 | Example

**Headline.** The 4 Line Page, filled in.
**Diagram.** A phone-width page skeleton.
```
Emergency dentist open Saturday
Open Saturday [your hours]
What counts as an emergency
  Cracked tooth. Knocked out tooth. Lost filling.
Emergency visit price range
  [your range]
Call Saturday [your number]
```
**Design note.** Bracketed placeholders in accent so nobody mistakes them for real figures.

## Slide 7 | Where to submit

**Headline.** Then ask Google and Bing to read it.
**Diagram.** Two stacked cards, each a tiny menu path.
Card A. Google Search Console, URL Inspection, paste the page address, Request indexing.
Card B. Bing Webmaster Tools, URL Submission, paste the page address, Submit.
**Design note.** Button names in accent.

## Slide 8 | Payoff

**Headline.** A page ChatGPT can point to.
**Diagram.** Seven filled dots and three empty dots, ten in a row, labeled "7 of 10".
**Text.** "The clinic that had the page was cited in 7 of 10 answers. ChatGPT, 2026-11-10."
**Design note.** Filled dots in accent. No promise, only the observed count.

## Slide 9 | Closing

**Series hook.** Next. The 6 directory pages ChatGPT cited. This clinic is listed on 2.
**CTA.** Comment SATURDAY and we'll send you the four line page outline, so you can publish it today.
**Diagram.** Six small page icons, two filled in accent.
**Footer.** Unbound GEO. Get named. Then PROVE it.
**Design note.** The footer is small and last.
