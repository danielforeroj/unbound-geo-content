# Example video script (60 seconds)

Built from `sample-report.md` (fictional). One idea. A missing emergency page.

**Format.** Vertical, 60 seconds. About 150 spoken words. Fix video, so the advice is tactical.
**CTA word.** SATURDAY.

---

**SCENE 1 | 0 to 7 s | [serious]**
SAY  This clinic has a 4.9 star rating. ChatGPT named it in 0 of 10 answers for a Saturday emergency dentist. Why?
SHOW Big "0 of 10" in the accent color. Small "4.9 stars" under it. Hold on the question.

**SCENE 2 | 7 to 17 s | [serious]**
SAY  Another clinic was named in 9 of 10. In 7 of those 10 answers, ChatGPT cited one page. A page called emergency dentist.
SHOW Two columns, "0 of 10" and "9 of 10". Then a page icon labeled "Emergency dentist" with "cited 7 of 10".

**SCENE 3 | 17 to 24 s | [amused]**
SAY  And the clinic with zero? Its Saturday hours are a picture in the footer. No text. No page.
SHOW Site footer mockup. The hours are an image. A red outline around it. A "no page" stamp.

**SCENE 4 | 24 to 44 s | [quick]**
SAY  So write one page with four lines. Saturday hours as plain text. What counts as an emergency. A price range. A phone number somebody answers on Saturday. Then open Google Search Console, paste the page address in the inspection bar, and hit request indexing.
SHOW Four lines appear one at a time and stay on screen in full.
  1. Saturday hours as plain text
  2. What counts as an emergency
  3. A price range
  4. A phone number answered on Saturday
  Then a screen recording of Search Console. URL inspection bar highlighted, then the "Request indexing" button.

**SCENE 5 | 44 to 51 s | [beat] then [serious]**
SAY  Then ask ChatGPT the same question 10 times in fresh chats and count. That's your starting line.
SHOW A tally of ten boxes, filled as it counts. "Named in N of 10".

**SCENE 6 | 51 to 58 s | [warm]**
SAY  Comment SATURDAY and we'll send you the four line outline, so you can publish it today. Unbound GEO. Get named. Then prove it.
SHOW "Comment SATURDAY" with SATURDAY in the accent color. Then the logo and "Get named. Then PROVE it."

---

**Length.** 150 spoken words, about 58 seconds at a natural pace.
**Lint.** No colons in spoken lines. No em or en dashes. No real business name. Brand once, last. Four lines shown in full in scene 4.
