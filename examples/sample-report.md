# Sample AI visibility report

> **FICTIONAL.** This report is invented for demonstration. The clinic, the city, the competitor and every number below do not exist. Do not quote any of it as real data.

**Brand tested.** Brightline Dental (fictional), a dental clinic in a mid-size US city.
**Engine.** ChatGPT.
**Date.** 2026-11-10.

Every line has an ID (R1, R2, ...) so content can cite it.

## Method

| Line | Fact |
|---|---|
| R1 | 5 questions a patient would type, each asked 10 times in fresh chats, 50 answers in total. Engine ChatGPT. Date 2026-11-10. |

## Named in, by question

"Named" means the clinic appears by name in the answer. Competitor A is the clinic named most often in this market.

| Line | Question | Brightline named in | Competitor A named in |
|---|---|---|---|
| R2 | Best dentist in town | 2 of 10 | 8 of 10 |
| R3 | Emergency dentist open Saturday | 0 of 10 | 9 of 10 |
| R4 | Affordable dental implants | 1 of 10 | 6 of 10 |
| R5 | Best dentist for anxious patients | 0 of 10 | 5 of 10 |
| R6 | Dentist that takes Delta Dental | 3 of 10 | 3 of 10 |

## Totals

| Line | Fact |
|---|---|
| R7 | Brightline named in 6 of 50 answers (12%). Competitor A named in 31 of 50 answers (62%). |

## Reviews

| Line | Fact |
|---|---|

## Pages ChatGPT cited

| Line | Fact |
|---|---|
| R9 | 14 distinct pages were cited across the 50 answers. 6 were directory or review pages, 4 were local news or community lists, 3 were competitor clinic sites, 1 was Brightline's own site. |
| R10 | For question 2 (Saturday emergency), Competitor A's emergency dentist page was cited in 7 of 10 answers. |
| R12 | Brightline is listed on 2 of the 6 directory or review pages cited. Competitor A is listed on 6 of 6. |
| R13 | Brightline is listed on 0 of the 4 local news or community lists cited. |

## Findings

| Line | Fact |
|---|---|
| R11 | Brightline's Saturday hours appear only as an image in the site footer. There is no emergency page. |

## Recommended fix

| Line | Fact |
|---|---|
| R14 | Publish one emergency page with four things on it. 1. Saturday hours as plain text. 2. What counts as an emergency. 3. A price range for an emergency visit. 4. A phone number answered on Saturday. Then request indexing in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools. |

| Line | Fact |
|---|---|
