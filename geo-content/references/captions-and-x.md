# Captions and X

The caption and the thread are not the script in writing. The video says the story. The caption and the thread carry what the video had no time for, which is method, caveats and context.

## Caption (Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, Shorts)

### Job

Add what the video could not hold. Never repeat the script.

### Structure

1. **Line 1, the method.** What was asked, how many times, which engine, which date. This is where rule 3 lives. Every number in the video must be traceable to this line.
2. **Line 2, the context.** One extra fact from the report the video did not use, or the caveat. Examples. How many pages the engine cited and where they came from. That answers vary run to run.
3. **The CTA.** The same word and outcome as the video. "Comment WORD and we'll send you X, so you can Y."
4. **Optional.** Up to 3 hashtags on their own line. Plain ones. #GEO #AISearch #ChatGPT.

Keep it short. Four to six lines in total. No paragraph walls.

### Rules

- The engine and the date appear next to the counts. "ChatGPT, tested 2026-11-10".
- Counts in the form "N of M". A percentage only with its count: "6 of 50 (12%)".
- No real business names. No "share of voice". No promise of rankings.
- No em or en dashes. US English.
- Do not restate the hook. Do not restate the fix steps. If the caption could replace the video, you repeated it.
- Colons are allowed in captions only for a link or a time. Avoid them anyway. They read as machine written.
- Brand once at most, last, as the sign-off line if at all.

### Template

```
How we tested. [Q] questions a [customer] would type, each asked [M] times in fresh [ENGINE] chats on [DATE]. [N] of [T] ([X%]) named [the clinic].
[One extra fact or caveat from the report.]
Comment WORD and we'll send you [X], so you can [Y].
#tag #tag #tag
```

## X (Twitter)

### Rules

- **One post per topic.** One video, one thread. Do not post three threads on the same finding.
- **Video in tweet 1.** Tweet 1 carries the video, with the hook as the text. It must stand alone.
- **The reasoning goes in the thread.** Method, evidence, the fix, the caveat, the CTA. Each tweet adds, none repeats.
- **One idea per tweet.** If a tweet has two ideas, split it.
- **280 characters or fewer per tweet.** Count them. Aim for 220 to leave room.
- **Thread length.** 5 to 8 tweets. More is a blog post.
- **Numbers.** "N of M". Percent only beside its count. The engine and date appear in tweet 2.
- **No numbering like "1/".** The platform shows thread structure.
- **No hashtags** in the thread, or one at most in the last tweet.
- **No links in tweet 1.** Put the link, if any, in the last tweet.
- **Plain sentences.** Short. No colons in the first tweet.

### Thread map

| Tweet | Job |
|---|---|
| 1 | The hook as text plus the video. One count. One question or contrast. |
| 2 | Method. What was asked, how many times, engine, date. |
| 3 | The main numbers. "N of M (X%)" for both sides. |
| 4 | What the engine cited. The citation count and its parts. |
| 5 | The fix, as a list, all items complete. |
| 6 | The literal how. Where to submit or who to email. |
| 7 | How to measure it again. Counts, fresh chats, same date format. |
| 8 | The caveat, then the CTA. |

Drop 4 or 7 for a shorter thread. Never drop 2 or the caveat.

### The caveat tweet

Always say, in plain words, that answers vary, the sample is small, and the numbers are one engine on one date. It protects the reader and it is what makes the rest believable.

### Reply CTA

On X the CTA is a reply, not a comment. "Reply WORD and we'll DM you X, so you can Y."

## Before returning

- The caption does not repeat the script.
- Every tweet is 280 characters or fewer.
- Tweet 1 has the video and stands alone.
- The caveat and the engine and date are present.
- Number check table is attached.
