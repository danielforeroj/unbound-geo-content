# Video script

A script for a vertical video of 45 seconds to 2 minutes. One idea. Spoken, not read.

## Length and word budget

Spoken pace for a natural, slightly quick delivery is about 2.5 words per second. Plan the words, then check the clock.

| Length | Spoken words | Scenes |
|---|---|---|
| 45 s | 110 to 120 | 5 |
| 60 s | 145 to 155 | 6 |
| 90 s | 220 to 235 | 7 to 8 |
| 2 min | 295 to 310 | 9 to 10 |

If the user gives no length, use 60 s.

## Scene structure

Every script has these beats. Short videos merge beats. They never drop the first, the fix, or the CTA.

| Beat | Share of time | Job |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Hook | 8 to 12 percent | Curiosity gap plus emotion. One to two sentences. See `hooks.md`. |
| 2. Proof | 15 to 20 percent | The count from the report. "Named in N of M". Engine and what was asked. |
| 3. Why | 12 to 18 percent | The cause the report shows. One cause only. |
| 4. Fix | 35 to 45 percent | The tactical how. What to write, where to submit, who to email, what to say. |
| 5. Check | 8 to 10 percent | How to see if it worked. One sentence, one action. |
| 6. CTA | 8 to 10 percent | Outcome CTA, then the brand, last. See `cta.md`. |

The 60 second sample in `examples/example-video-script.md` runs as: hook 0 to 7 s, proof 7 to 17 s, why 17 to 24 s, fix 24 to 44 s, check 44 to 51 s, CTA 51 to 58 s.

## Spoken line rules

- Conversational. Write how a person talks to one friend.
- No colons in any spoken line. Replace with a period. "Four things. One." not "Four things: one".
- Short sentences. Aim for 4 to 12 words. One sentence over 16 words per script, tops.
- Ask, then answer. "So what do you write? One page, four lines." beats "You should write one page with four lines."
- Contractions are good. "We'll", "it's", "that's".
- Numbers said as people say them. "Nine of ten" is fine on screen as "9 of 10". Keep the digits in the script so the check table can match.
- No jargon without a plain gloss. "Cited" means "ChatGPT pointed to this page". Say it once.
- Value first, brand last. The brand name appears in the last scene only. If you can delete the brand line and the video still works, it is placed right.
- One idea. If a scene serves a second idea, cut it. It goes in a different video.

## The fix scene

This is the scene people save. Give the literal how.

- **What to write.** The exact elements, in order.
- **Where to submit.** The product and the button. "Google Search Console, URL Inspection, Request indexing."
- **Who to email.** The role, and the one thing to ask. "Email the editor of the local list and ask to be considered for the next update."
- **What to say.** If there is a form or a message, say the words.

Match the video's kind. A fix video gives fix advice. A measurement video gives measurement advice. A video about measuring does not end with "now go publish a page".

When you count, show all of it. If the voice says "four things", the screen shows four lines, in full, in order, for long enough to read (about 1 second per 3 words, plus half a second).

## Tone tags (optional)

Tags are delivery notes for the person on camera or the voice track. They are not spoken. Use at most one per scene, and only where the emotion changes.

- `[serious]` stakes, loss, the number.
- `[amused]` a wry beat, a small absurdity.
- `[warm]` the CTA, the offer.
- `[quick]` a list or a fast how.
- `[beat]` a one second pause before the answer.

If the user did not ask for tags, still include them on the hook and the CTA. They cost nothing and help a voice actor.

## Format to return

Use one block per scene.

```
SCENE 2 | 7 to 17 s | [serious]
SAY    Another clinic was named in 9 of 10. In 7 of those 10 answers, ChatGPT cited one page.
SHOW   Two columns. "9 of 10" lit in accent color. A page icon with "7 of 10".
```

Then, at the end, the total word count, the estimated length, and the number check table.

## Closing

Last scene is always the same shape.

1. The outcome CTA. "Comment WORD and we'll send you X, so you can Y."
2. The brand and the slogan, one breath. "Unbound GEO. Get named. Then prove it."

No "like and subscribe". No "thanks for watching". No "see you in the next one". Never close with a sign-off that says this is the last chance.

## Final check for any script

- Read the whole script out loud. Mark where you breathe wrong. Cut there.
- Hook under 25 words.
- Zero colons in spoken lines. Zero em or en dashes.
- Every number has an R# line. Counts written "N of M".
- Every counted list is on screen in full.
- Brand appears once, in the last scene.
- No real business names. No "share of voice". No ranking promise.
